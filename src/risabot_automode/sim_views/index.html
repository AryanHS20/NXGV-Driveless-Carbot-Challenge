    Run your first simulation

    1. Click the 20 cm UWB preset to try the new local guidance, or leave Baseline selected.
    2. Click Run both missions.
    3. Select a view from the dropdown at the top of either panel.
    4. Choose Three camera feeds on the left and Warp + stitch + road mask on the right to inspect perception.
    5. Choose Actual vs estimated pose and Local planner to inspect navigation.
    6. Use 4× to run faster, or Pause and Single step to inspect a decision.
    7. At completion, select Assessment graphs and click Export run CSV.

    The first mission ends at the traffic-light approach. The second continues from that pose and parks in the parallel bay. A light or gate changes in the environment; the controller responds only to observed state.

    V3 navigation: camera boundaries correct local drift and guide the route-conditioned corridor. UWB runs continuously in a separate coarse estimate; its corrections never move the local steering pose. Violet on the map is the coarse estimate; yellow is the local navigation pose. Camera freeze still stops the car. This model uses a known starting pose and a drawing-derived prior map.

    Simulation scope: actual 3D rendered cameras, pixel-based lane extraction, independent noisy pose estimation, full-footprint planning, Reeds–Shepp parking, actuator lag, terrain-following pitch/roll, and Rapier obstacle collision checks. This is not a calibrated tyre, suspension or traction model.
