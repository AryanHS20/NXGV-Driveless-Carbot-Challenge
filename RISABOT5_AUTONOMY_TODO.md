## Acceptance and evidence conventions

The repeat counts here are proposed engineering acceptance criteria, not quoted competition rules. Confirm exact course/scoring requirements when testing the full mission. Determine tolerances from measured geometry; do not assume a 5 cm error is acceptable on a tight lane.

For a straight lane, a starting lateral error allowance is `(measured drivable width - vehicle width) / 2 - chosen clearance margin`. It must be positive; bends require a swept-body check. Set tighter tracking/calibration targets within that allowance. Measure real stop distance at the requested duty rather than assuming duty equals speed.

Each completed item needs: UTC time, vehicle ID, Git HEAD plus working-file hashes, profile/model/config hashes, test ID, initial condition, expected behavior, measured result, PASS/FAIL, and raw evidence path (bag/images/video/log/measurement). Record failures and retest after relevant changes. A unit-test PASS, dashboard green indicator or simulator lap does not replace a physical test.
